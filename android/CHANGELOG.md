# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

First Android release.

- Download video or MP3 from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X and 1,000+ other sites.
- Share → YTDD from any app: a bottom panel starts the download immediately, and you can hide it.
- Paste & download from the clipboard, or type a link.
- Every video is an MP4 that plays everywhere (H.264 + AAC); other formats are converted on the phone.
- Saved to the gallery (Movies/YTDD, Music/YTDD).
- WhatsApp (status or chat) and Share buttons on every download, in the app, the notification and the widget.
- Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile, and long-press shortcuts.
- Download engine (yt-dlp) updates itself on every launch, and retries once after updating if a download fails.
- Dark by default, with Light and Match phone options.
